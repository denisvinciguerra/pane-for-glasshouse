    Advanced
    Power & standby
    Quick Boot
    Switching off puts the TV into a light sleep rather than shutting it down, so it comes back on faster. LG calls this Quick Start+.
    Stay connected when off
    Keeps the TV on the network while it is switched off, apart from five hours each night, so this dashboard and Home Assistant keep working and can switch it back on. The screen stays dark. Uses about 12 W on an LG C2, against almost nothing in normal standby.
    Always Ready
    Switched off with the remote, the TV shows LG's Always Ready screen, such as a clock or artwork, instead of going dark, from the next time it is switched off. Uses about 30 W on an LG C2, close to what it uses when on. The first time, the TV asks for OK on the remote. Home turns the TV back on from the clock; the power button switches it fully off.
    Nightly power-down
    For five hours every night, LG lets a switched-off TV sleep fully. It is offline then: this dashboard and Home Assistant can't reach it until the hours end or it is switched on. LG fixes the length; choose hours when the TV isn't used.
    Wake-on-LAN
    Lets Home Assistant, a phone or another device on the network switch the TV on from standby. Requires Quick Boot (Quick Start+) to keep the network connection responsive while off. The Home Assistant guide shows how to set it up. LG calls this Mobile TV On.
    Timers
    On Timer
    Switches the TV on at this time, once or on the days picked.
    Switched on this way, the TV goes back off after two hours unless a button is pressed. That can be turned off in the TV's own Timers menu.
    Off Timer
    Switches the TV off at this time, once or on the days picked.
    Screen time
    Screen-time limits
    Limits how long the screen is on each day, in all and per app or input, and keeps it off at bedtime. The TV shows a warning five minutes before a limit, and another when it is reached, then does what is chosen below a minute later.
    Daily limit
    The screen time allowed each day, counted from midnight on the TV’s clock.
    Bedtime
    Between these two times, the TV does what is chosen below. Clearing either time turns bedtime off.
    At the daily limit or bedtime
    App and input limits
    Minutes a day for one app or input. Those watched in the last 30 days can be picked.
    At an app or input’s limit
    System updates
    Block TV software updates
    Stops the TV installing LG's system updates, which can take away the access Glasshouse runs with. Apps and Glasshouse's own updates are unaffected.
    Display
    12-hour clock
    Shows the time as 9:40 PM rather than 21:40 on the TV dashboard, Glasshouse Home and the clock screen savers.
    LG logo
    Shows the LG logo as the TV switches on and off. LG calls this LG Logo Display.
    Ambient light
    PicCap capture
    Sends what is on screen to PicCap's Hyperion server, which drives an ambient light behind the TV. Off stops the capture, and the light with it.
    Hue Sync streaming
    Starts and stops the colour stream the Hue Sync app sends to a Philips Ambience light. Off stops the stream, and the light with it.
    HDMI inputs
    Devices
    Auto device detection
    Lets the TV find devices at home that it can show on its Home Dashboard and control: a set-top box on an HDMI input, and smart lights, plugs and switches on your network. On newer TVs it looks up every address on your home network each time the TV switches on, which a network-wide ad blocker such as Pi-hole shows as hundreds of requests. Switched off, the scans stop and the TV no longer finds devices by itself.

    Ad & telemetry blocker
    Stops the TV reaching LG's ad, tracking and diagnostics servers.
    —
    LG's apps, such as the Content Store and LG Channels, can take about a minute to connect again. If one shows an error, exit it and open it again after a minute.
    If an app or one of LG's services stops working while the blocker is on, switch it off to check whether it is the cause.
    Everything also blocks LG's platform services, so the LG ThinQ app can no longer control the TV, and the app store, software updates and LG Channels may stop working. Where it blocks LG's search, Glasshouse Home's search is hidden until the blocker is back on Ads & telemetry or off. To stop only system updates, use Block TV software updates in the Advanced tab.

    Dashboard port
    The port this dashboard answers on, 8080 unless changed. Change it if another app on the TV needs 8080, such as Stremio. Saving restarts the server, and this page follows to the new address.
    Prometheus metrics
    Lets a Prometheus server on the network collect the TV's readings, at /api/prometheus/metrics. While off, the address does not exist.
    Web app debugger
    In Developer Mode, lets developer tools on the network inspect the TV's web apps without an SSH tunnel. webOS leaves it open with no password, so keep it closed unless it is needed.

    SSH keys
    The public keys that can log in to the TV as root over SSH. While any key file is there, the Homebrew Channel leaves root without a password, so only these keys get in.
    Changing these needs a token set in config.json. Without one, anyone on the network could add their own key.

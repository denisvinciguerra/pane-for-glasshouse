Pane for Glasshouse 1.1.0

  • Global shortcuts: give a button, an input or an action a key combination that works in any app, with a small card under the menu-bar icon. Set them in Settings › Keys or from the popover's keyboard icon.
  • Click the keyboard icon, then a button, to set its popover key and its global shortcut from the bottom bar.
  • A pinned action triggered while the TV is off, from its tile, its key or its global shortcut, turns it on first, then runs.
  • Edit the pins on Remote: the pencil next to Pinned adds a pin, deletes one with its keys, or moves it by dragging.
  • A pinned app tile is greyed evenly while the TV is off.

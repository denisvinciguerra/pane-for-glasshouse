Pane for Glasshouse 1.0.0

The first public release.

  • A remote for your LG TV in the menu bar: remote control buttons and sources.
  • Picture, Audio and Game settings, drawn from what the server sends.
  • HDMI ports.
  • Power from any state, including LG's Always Ready screen, and Wake-on-LAN for a TV in deep standby.
  • Live statistics from the TV.
  • Several TVs, found by searching the network, by address or from a dashboard link. Pinned settings and custom actions, with their keys, are kept for each TV. A pin that launches an app can show the app's icon.
  • Keyboard keys while the popover is open: Arrows, Gaming and Custom profiles, shared by all TVs.
  • Updates through Sparkle, with a notification when one is available.

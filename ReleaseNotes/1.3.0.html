Pane for Glasshouse 1.3.0

  • Apps: a new tab, second after Remote, lists every app on the TV, system apps included: click one to launch it, or open the list with "Pin…" and pin any app in one click.
  • Pointer: when the TV's Glasshouse offers it (0.146 or later, Pointer input turned on in Server › Experimental features), the Remote's Pointer key swaps the d-pad for a trackpad. Click and drag, or hold ⌥ and move the mouse, as picked above the pad; click to select, right-click to go back, scroll with the wheel or two fingers. Contributed by Kirill Klenovich (@clima365).
  • Pointer: type on the TV from the pointer's pad. Letters light up as they arrive and fade out ten seconds later; Return submits, Esc clears. Space with nothing typed still plays or pauses.
  • Remote: the classic keys show a fine-line icon instead of a word, drawn after Glasshouse's own remote; the word shows on hover and for VoiceOver.
  • A new look: the d-pad is a ring, as on Glasshouse's own remote; keys, menus and fields have rounded corners and softer edges; names and numbers are set in Outfit; settings get round-thumb sliders, switches, and group titles over a rule.
  • The Picture tab's mode menu takes its own width, so a long mode name is no longer cut short.
  • The search for TVs no longer reports local network access as blocked only because the SSDP search was refused while the subnet probes went through.
